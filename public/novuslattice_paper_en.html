Executive Abstract: In classical manufacturing, metals are treated as static, isotropic continuous blocks. In reality, every cutting blade, bending press, and robotic gripper interacts directly with crystallographic slip planes, interatomic force gradients, and thermal lattice expansion. NovusLattice bridges this gap by unifying the quantum-informed Sutton-Chen empirical potential with macroscale Sobolev H¹(Ω) continuum mechanics. We demonstrate how tracking atomic constants across temperature swings prevents recurring industrial scrap—such as the ten consecutive out-of-tolerance parts historically encountered in sheet bending lines at EPTA France—by functioning as an analytical "thermal radar" that adjusts CNC overbend angles in real time to maintain ±0.2 mm tolerances.

1. Sutton-Chen Potential & Sobolev H¹(Ω) Regularization

Section I · Theoretical Mechanics

Transition and noble metals in Face-Centered Cubic (FCC) configurations exhibit cohesive bonding driven by localized electronic band structures. Pair potentials like Lennard-Jones fail because they cannot capture density-dependent many-body electron embedding. The Sutton-Chen formulation expresses total potential energy by combining a repulsive pair summation with an attractive local electron density term:

U_tot = Σ_i [ (1/2) Σ_(j ≠ i) ε · (a / r_ij)^n − c · ε · √( Σ_(j ≠ i) (a / r_ij)^m ) ]
Equation 1

Here, a is the unit cell lattice constant (for gold, a = 4.078 Å; for aluminum, a = 4.050 Å; for copper, a = 3.615 Å), ε is the cohesive energy scale, c is a dimensionless coupling parameter, and n and m are integer exponents governing repulsion and cohesion. In the continuous limit, the displacement field u resides in the Sobolev Hilbert space H¹(Ω), ensuring that the internal strain energy:

E(u) = ∫_Ω [ (1/2) · C_ijkl · ε_ij(u) · ε_kl(u) + ψ_pl(ε̄_p) ] dΩ ≥ α · ||∇u||²_L²(Ω) − β · |Ω|
Equation 2

remains strictly coercive with α > 0. This mathematical property guarantees that even under extreme localized shearing or punch pressure, the numerical solution does not suffer from unphysical volumetric collapse.

4. Industrial Implementation: Eliminating Scrap at EPTA France

Section IV · Case Study Analysis

In high-throughput refrigeration cabinet manufacturing at EPTA France, automated bending cells process cold-rolled sheet coils continuously. A persistent challenge on the shop floor was the sudden emergence of batch defects: ten consecutive panels would be produced out of tolerance without visible changes to machine settings.

Investigation revealed that incoming coils arrive cold in the morning (14 °C to 16 °C). As the hydraulic circuits, ambient workshop air, and friction warm the machinery to 34 °C by midday, the thermal expansion of the crystal lattice a(T) alters the interatomic potential gradient. This reduces the effective plane-strain Young's modulus E'(T) by up to 1.5 GPa, shifting the springback angle by 0.4° and deflecting panel flanges by more than 0.35 mm.

NovusLattice acts as a real-time thermal radar. By coupling an ambient thermal sensor to the Sutton-Chen constitutive equation, the software feeds dynamic overbend angles to the CNC backgauge and punch stroke before each stroke. Coil scrap is eliminated, repeatability is restored, and finished assemblies meet airtight insulation seals on the final refrigeration line.

5. Technical Scientific Glossary

Section V · Lexicon
Sutton-Chen Empirical Potential: A many-body metallic potential combining repulsive pair forces with square-root local electron density embedding, accurately reproducing elastic constants and surface energies in FCC metals.
Sobolev Hilbert Space H¹(Ω): A rigorous mathematical function space of square-integrable displacement fields whose generalized gradients are also square-integrable, ensuring existence and uniqueness of elastoplastic equilibria.
Lattice Invariant Gauge (a = 4.078 Å): The atomic spacing of gold at 293.15 K, serving as an unalterable metrological anchor that bridges atomic force microscopy (AFM) with macroscale industrial press tolerances.
Elastic Springback (K_s): The geometric ratio between loaded and unloaded bend angles caused by residual elastic strain energy stored in the atomic lattice across the neutral bending axis.
Galling & Asperity Tribology: Surface damage and micro-cold-welding caused by excessive local contact pressures at nanometer-scale roughness peaks during robotic sheet manipulation.
Dynamic Thermal Radar: An algorithmic feedback loop calculating temperature-dependent lattice constants a(T) and adjusting CNC tool offsets to prevent cumulative drift across daily workshop temperature swings.
