1. Executive Summary & Physical Foundations

Historically, precision manufacturing and metal forming industries have modeled materials through static, homogeneous linear empirical coefficients. However, for tight-tolerance industrial parts such as commercial refrigeration chassis at EPTA France, strict specifications of ±0.2 mm are systematically breached. The underlying cause is not mechanical slop, but atomic physics: dislocation cascades along crystallographic {111} slip planes, stored elastic bond energy, and daily workshop thermal shifts alter the material's elasto-plastic response.

NovusLattice, conceived and formulated by Mikel Irastorza, unifies the many-body physics of FCC crystal lattices (featuring the canonical gold lattice constant a = 4.078 Å and aluminum a = 4.049 Å) with nonlinear continuum mechanics in Hilbert-Sobolev spaces H¹(Ω). By deploying the Cauchy-Born kinematic rule coupled with real-time workshop thermal radar compensation, NovusLattice eliminates serial scrap and out-of-tolerance rejects.

2. Unified Mathematical Formulation (Sutton-Chen Potential)

The total cohesive energy of the metallic lattice is computed by combining short-range Born-Mayer pair repulsion with long-range multi-body electronic d-band attraction (Finnis-Sinclair):

U_total = ε · [ 1/2 · Σ_i Σ_{j≠i} (a / r_ij)^n - c · Σ_i √( ρ_i ) ] ρ_i = Σ_{j≠i} (a / r_ij)^m

Where ε is the cohesive energy scale (eV), a is the equilibrium lattice constant (4.078 Å in Au, 4.049 Å in Al), n and m are integer range exponents (n=10, m=8 for Au; n=9, m=6 for Ni), c is the dimensionless many-body coupling parameter, and r_ij is interatomic separation.

4. The 4 Canonical Industrial Use Cases (EPTA France Benchmark)

Case 1: Precision Shearing & Blanking Clearance

Improper clearance causes burr formation and tool edge degradation. NovusLattice calculates the optimal blade clearance by minimizing interatomic fracture energy along {111} planes: Optimal Clearance C_opt = 0.006 · √(σ_y) · t = 0.139 mm (±5 µm) for 1.5 mm sheet, ensuring clean ductile rupture without micro-cracks.

Case 2: Precision Bending & Springback Compensation (EPTA France Benchmark)

At EPTA France, a 90° bend exhibits a springback of 0.934°, shifting the bent flange by 0.815 mm—over four times the allowable ±0.2 mm tolerance. NovusLattice integrates the elasto-plastic stress distribution and commands the CNC brake to overbend to 90.934°. Upon tool release, the flange rebounds to precisely 90.000° with an error of only 0.025 mm (10x tighter than required).

Case 3: Robotic Gripping, Tribology & Anti-Galling Protection

High-speed dry contact between robotic gripper pads and sheet metal induces severe friction galling. NovusLattice calculates the critical normal contact force threshold of 69.3 kN over 400 mm² at friction coefficient μ = 0.35, preventing cold-welding and surface score marks.

Case 4: Workshop Environmental Drift & Real-Time Thermal Radar

Between a 15°C morning and a 35°C afternoon, thermal lattice expansion a(T) and modulus degradation E(T) shift large-part dimensions by 0.58 mm. The NovusLattice thermal radar dynamically recalculates punch penetration depth (+0.082 mm at noon), achieving zero scrap across the entire production shift.
