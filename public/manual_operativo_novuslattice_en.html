NovusLattice: Industrial Operations & Implementation Manual

Practical guide for machine operators, manufacturing engineers, and CNC programmers: thermal radar calibration, springback overbend compensation (±0.2 mm tolerance), and burr-free shear cutting.

1. Operational Summary & Shop-Floor Purpose

Section I · Introduction

In precision sheet metal manufacturing (commercial refrigeration, automotive chassis, and mechatronic enclosures), scrap caused by trial bends and temperature-induced dimensional drift represents a major hidden cost. NovusLattice replaces trial-and-error adjustments with exact physical modeling grounded in atomic crystal lattice mechanics and real-time workshop temperature tracking.

Installation & System Setup ZERO OVERHEAD

Runs on any workshop PC or machine terminal (Linux, Windows, or macOS) with Python 3.9+. Operates 100% offline with zero cloud dependency.

git clone https://github.com/novomundus2-cmyk/NovusLattice.git && cd NovusLattice && pip install -e .

2. Precision Sheet Bending Procedure (EPTA France Benchmark)

Section II · CNC Press Brake

To eliminate elastic recovery (springback) scrap and consistently hold strict engineering tolerances (±0.20 mm):

Step 1: Measure Coil & Workshop Temperature THERMAL SENSOR

Record the incoming metal coil and ambient air temperature (e.g., 20 °C in the morning, 35 °C in the afternoon) using an infrared pyrometer or the machine's ambient RTD probe.

Step 2: Execute Real-Time Overbend Calculation CLI COMMAND

Input the alloy type, sheet thickness (mm), punch radius, and temperature:

python3 -m novuslattice.cli bend --metal Al --thickness 1.5 --angle 90.0 --temp-k 293.15

Calculated output: Target punch stroke angle = 90.934°. Final residual error = 0.025 mm (well within the ±0.20 mm benchmark).

Step 3: Transfer Angle Correction to CNC Controller MACHINE OFFSET

Apply the +0.93° overbend correction on the press brake controller (Amada, Trumpf, Bystronic, or LVD) on the Y1/Y2 axis. The first produced part releases at exactly 90.0°, eliminating scrap panels.

3. Burr-Free Industrial Shearing & Cutting

Section III · Shear Cutting

Incorrect blade clearance causes ductile tearing and secondary burrs that require costly manual deburring. NovusLattice computes the exact clearance based on {111} crystallographic slip planes:

Sheet Alloy Thickness (t) Optimal Blade Clearance Tooling Tolerance Operational Advantage
Aluminum 6061 1.5 mm 0.139 mm (9.3% of t) ±0.005 mm (±5 µm) Clean shear separation with zero burr
Stainless Steel 304 1.2 mm 0.144 mm (12.0% de t) ±0.005 mm (±5 µm) Suppresses work-hardening at the edge
Electrolytic Copper 2.0 mm 0.193 mm (9.6% de t) ±0.008 mm (±8 µm) Sharp rectangular edges for busbars

4. Robotic Gripper Calibration & Anti-Galling Bounds

Section IV · Robotics & Tribology

When automated transfer arms or mobile companion robots (MIRA Robot Lab) handle finished sheet panels:

Clamping Force Limit to Prevent Surface Galling SURFACE INTEGRITY

Excessive contact pressure at microscopic asperities causes cold-welding and scratching on pre-painted or polished metal. NovusLattice sets safe normal load limits:

python3 -m novuslattice.cli cases

For 400 mm² contact pads, maximum normal clamping force in copper must not exceed 69.3 kN with a static friction coefficient of 0.35.

5. Shop-Floor Troubleshooting Guide

Section V · Rapid Resolution
Issue: Parts open by 1° more in the afternoon than in the morning. Root Cause: Workshop warmed from 15 °C to 34 °C, thermally expanding lattice a(T) and softening modulus E(T).
Action: Re-run CLI with current temperature and increase press overbend by +0.35°.
Issue: Sharp burrs appear along bottom edge of cut sheet. Root Cause: Blade clearance is too wide; material bends instead of shearing.
Action: Re-shim knife gap to exact NovusLattice specification (0.139 mm for Al 1.5mm).
Issue: Robotic suction or metal clamp leaves cosmetic indentation marks. Root Cause: Asperity contact pressure exceeds material yield limit σ_y.
Action: Reduce pneumatic pressure or enlarge pad surface area to 400 mm².
Issue: New sheet coil batch exhibits bend angle variation. Root Cause: Inter-batch yield strength fluctuation from raw material supplier.
Action: Measure a single witness bend, feed deviation into NovusLattice, and apply correction across the entire coil.
